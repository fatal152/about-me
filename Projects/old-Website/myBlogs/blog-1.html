The making of this page

I made a website months ago on some random hosting site and then completely forgot about it. Well, not completely forgot, but I definitely stopped working on it. I’d plugged it everywhere, but eventually, I realized it needed a serious overhaul. It was rusty, to say the least.

So, I decided to create a blog section to showcase my projects. Fast forward to today, I got motivated to start and realized I didn't have Visual Code installed. When I finally got it set up, I looked at my old code and spent a solid four minutes regretting my past self's decisions. I eventually jumped in, but then realized I had no clue what I was doing. A quick crash course later, I was ready to start.

My first goal was to create a centered div. The crash course was no help, so I had to Google it. Turns out, it wasn't as complicated as I thought. After that, everything went smoothly until I hit a writer's block. I couldn’t just leave an empty page, so I came up with this.

Hi there! Welcome to my first blog post. I’m new to blogging, but hey, everyone starts somewhere, right? Hopefully, you found this at least a little interesting. If not, well, I hope you enjoyed the read!
